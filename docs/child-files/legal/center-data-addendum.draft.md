# Child Records Data Addendum (DRAFT)

**DRAFT for attorney review. Not legal advice. Not in force.** Text in [brackets] is a decision for you and
your attorney. This addendum would be accepted by the center's owner in the app before Child Files is
switched on for that center, and recorded with the date, the person and the version.

## 1. Purpose and definitions
This addendum applies when a Center uses Child Files in CareLocal. It adds to the Terms & Conditions; if they
conflict about Child Records, this addendum controls.

- **Center**: the licensed child care center that holds the account.
- **CareLocal**: [legal entity name].
- **Child Records**: information about enrolled or prospective children and their parents, guardians and
  authorized pick-up contacts that the Center or a parent adds to CareLocal, including names, dates of birth,
  contact details, health, immunization, allergy and medical action plan information, custody or pick-up
  documents, and uploaded files.

## 2. Roles
1. **The Center owns and controls Child Records.** It decides what to collect, who sees it and how long it is kept.
2. **CareLocal processes Child Records on the Center's behalf**, only to provide Child Files and as the Center
   instructs through the product and these terms.
3. CareLocal will not sell Child Records, use them for advertising or marketing, or use them to train
   models [or: combine them with other data], and will not look at their contents except as needed to provide
   support the Center asks for, to keep the service secure, or as the law requires.

## 3. The Center's responsibilities
1. Collect Child Records lawfully and tell parents what is collected and why. The Center, not CareLocal, has the
   relationship with parents.
2. Keep them accurate, and update custody and pick-up information promptly. A stale pick-up list is a safety
   risk, and the Center is responsible for acting on current information.
3. Restrict access. Child Records can be opened only by the Center's owner and administrators. The Center decides
   which employees are promoted to administrator and is responsible for their conduct.
4. Keep records as the NC rules require (10A NCAC 09 .2318: while the child is enrolled and 1 year after) and
   delete them afterward. CareLocal flags files due for deletion; **the Center confirms the deletion.**
5. Answer parent requests to see, correct or delete records. CareLocal will help [within X business days].
6. Not upload more than is needed. In particular, no information about children's health beyond what a rule or the
   child's care requires.

## 4. Security
CareLocal will protect Child Records with: private file storage opened only through short-lived signed links;
access limited by role in the database; a log of every document view; encryption in transit [and at rest, confirm with
each subprocessor]; separate storage from staff documents; and secrets kept out of the code. It will review who has
production access [every X months]. The Center must keep its own sign-in details private and use [two-step
verification for owners and administrators once available].

## 5. Subprocessors
CareLocal uses: Supabase (database and sign-in), Cloudflare (file storage), Vercel (hosting), Resend (email),
Twilio (text messages and codes) and Stripe (billing, not Child Records). CareLocal will give [30 days] notice
before adding a subprocessor that will handle Child Records and the Center may object.

## 6. Security incidents
1. CareLocal will notify the Center's owner **immediately after discovering** an incident that exposes Child
   Records, and in any case within [24 hours], by email and phone to the contacts on the account (G.S. 75-65
   requires a business that only maintains data to tell the owner immediately).
2. CareLocal will tell the Center what it knows: what happened, which records and how many children, what has been
   done, and what the Center should do.
3. The **Center decides and sends** notices to families and to the Attorney General's Consumer Protection Division
   and, if over 1,000 people, the consumer reporting agencies. CareLocal will supply the facts and [reasonable help
   and a draft notice]. [Allocation of notification costs: counsel to decide.]

## 7. Ending and deletion
On request or when the Center closes its account, CareLocal will let the Center **export** its Child Records and
then **delete** them, including stored files, within [30] days, except what the law requires CareLocal to keep.
Backups are overwritten within [X days]. The Center is responsible for keeping any records the NC rules require it
to keep, so it must export before deleting.
