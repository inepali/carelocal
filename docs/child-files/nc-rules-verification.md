## Verified rules

### Record retention: 10A NCAC 09 .2318
- Records must be available **at the center during the hours of operation on the license** (.2318(1)).
- Records may be paper or electronic **if all required signatures are preserved in paper, PDF or other graphic format** (.2318(2)). A typed name stored in a database field is not, on its face, a preserved signature.
- Medication administration records: kept while the medication is given **and for 6 months after** (.2318(3)).
- Building inspections: while the license is valid. Fire, sanitation and pool inspections: at least 3 years (.2318(4), (5)).
- **Child records** (.2318(6)): the table lists **16 record types**, each marked "in each child's file for as long as the child is enrolled" and "for 1 year after the child is no longer enrolled" (32 marks for 16 rows; confirm visually on PDF page 91 before encoding retention per type):

### Application and emergency records: .0801 and .0802
- **Application for enrollment**: signed by the parent, **on file on the child's first day**. Contents: emergency medical information, full name and the name the child is called, date of birth, allergies with symptoms and response, health care needs, fears or behavior characteristics, and **the names of individuals the center may release the child to** (.0801(a)). Staff may release a child only to a person listed (.0801(c)).
- **Medical action plan**: attached to the application for a child with allergies, asthma or other chronic conditions; **updated annually and whenever it changes** (.0801(b)).
- Application information must be accessible to caregiving staff while the child is in care (.0801(d)).
- **Emergency medical care information**: parent contact, chosen health care professional, chronic illness and medication, other relevant information. **On file on the first day; updated as changes occur and at least annually** (.0802(c)).
- **Signed emergency treatment authorization**: on file on the first day, easily accessible (.0802(d)).
- **Incident report**: each time a child is injured; signed by the person completing it and by the parent; a copy goes to the parent (.0802(e)). If treatment by a health care professional is required, a copy is **mailed to the Division within 7 calendar days** (.0802(f)). An **incident log** is kept in a separate cumulative file (.0802(g)).

### Immunizations: G.S. 130A-155
- The child has **30 calendar days from the first day of attendance** to obtain required immunizations. A physician may certify that more days are needed for a multi-dose series.
- If still not obtained, **the operator "shall not permit the child to attend"** until it is. On the first day, with no certificate, the operator gives the parent a **notice of deficiency**.
- The facility **files an immunization report annually** with the Department. The report is **counts only**: children attending, number not immunized within 30 days, number with a medical exemption, number with a religious exemption. There is no per-child export. The statute gives no due date for child care facilities.
- Records are kept on file for every child and are open to inspection by the Department and the local health department.
- Exemption criteria are **not** in this section (not verified).

### Health assessment: G.S. 110-91(1)
- "Each child shall have a health assessment **before being admitted or within 30 days following admission**."
- May be done by a licensed physician, the physician's authorized agent approved by the NC Medical Board, a certified nurse practitioner, a licensed physician assistant (second version only) or a public health nurse meeting EPSDT standards.
- Written **religious objection** exempts a child who is and has been in normal health.

### Breach notice: G.S. 75-65
- A business that **owns or licenses** the data (the center) notifies affected people **without unreasonable delay**. No fixed number of days.
- A business that **only maintains** the data (CareLocal) notifies the owner **immediately following discovery**.
- Whenever affected people are notified, the notifying business also notifies the **Attorney General's Consumer Protection Division** without unreasonable delay, with: nature of the breach, number of consumers affected, steps taken to investigate and to prevent a recurrence, and the timing, distribution and content of the notice. If **more than 1,000** people are notified, the **consumer reporting agencies** are also notified.
- The required notice content includes a description of the incident, the data involved, protective steps, and FTC and Attorney General contact information.

## Corrections to the due-diligence PDF

| PDF says | Verified |
|---|---|
| "Five child records" on file | **16 record types** are retained (table above) |
| Action plan cited as ".0802(e)" | .0802(e) is the **incident report**. The action plan is **.0801(b)** |
| Emergency information renews "when info changes" | **At least annually**, and as changes occur (.0802(c)) |
| Application due "before or at enrollment" | **First day of attendance** (.0801(a)) |
| Medical report "per rule schedule", "not for school-age" | Deadline is **before admission or within 30 days after** (G.S. 110-91(1)); **school-age exemption not verified** |
| "Medication permission: center rules" | A state rule: .0803(3), (4), (6)-(9), (11), retained per .2318 |
| "Annual immunization report" as a ready-made feature | The report is **counts on a Department form**, not a per-child export |
| Daily arrival and departure times, cited ".0802(a)" | .0802(a) is the emergency plan; the attendance rule was **not located** |
